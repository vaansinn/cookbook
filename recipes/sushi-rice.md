# Sushi Rice

**Cuisine:** Japanese · **Serves:** 2 · **Time:** ~1 h (incl. resting)
**Tags:** japanese, rice, base-recipe

## Prep ahead
- Rinse and drain the rice first — the 15–30 min drain is passive time, use it to prep your fillings
- Measure vinegar, sugar and salt while the rice cooks, so the seasoning is ready the moment the rice rests
- Set out a wide bowl and a fan (a magazine works) before the rice finishes

## Ingredients
- 300 g short-grain sushi rice (about 1½ US cups)
- 330 ml water (ratio ~1:1.1 by weight)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions
1. Rinse the rice in 4–5 changes of water until it runs nearly clear.
2. Drain and let sit 15–30 min.
3. Cook with 330 ml water (slightly less than for regular rice — it still absorbs the vinegar later).
4. Rest covered 10 min off the heat.
5. Warm vinegar, sugar and salt just until dissolved — use the emptied rice pot or a short microwave burst, no extra saucepan needed.
6. Fold the seasoning through the hot rice with a cutting motion while fanning it.

## Notes & tweaks
- Yields ~700 g cooked rice — enough for 6 rolls or a generous nigiri plate for two.
- Equipment: one pot + one wide bowl is all you need.
