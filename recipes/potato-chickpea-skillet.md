# Spanish Potato-Chickpea Skillet with Feta

**Cuisine:** Spanish/Mediterranean · **Serves:** 2 · **Time:** ~30 min
**Tags:** mediterranean, potatoes, chickpeas, tomato-based, vegetarian

## Prep ahead
- Dice the potatoes small (~1.5 cm) so they cook through quickly
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas
- Chop the onion and garlic

## Ingredients
- 500 g potatoes (festkochend), diced small
- 1 can chickpeas (400 g), drained
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- ½ lemon, to finish
- Fresh parsley (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet and fry the diced potatoes over medium heat until starting to brown and nearly tender, ~12–15 min.
2. Add the onion and cook 4 min, then the garlic, paprika and cumin, 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add the chickpeas and chopped tomatoes. Simmer 8–10 min until the potatoes are fully tender and the sauce thickened.
4. Squeeze over the lemon, taste and season. Crumble the feta on top, scatter parsley, and serve from the pan.

## Notes & tweaks
- A fried or poached egg on top makes it more substantial.
- Smoked paprika (pimentón) is what gives it the Spanish character — worth it over regular paprika.
- Equipment: one large skillet.
