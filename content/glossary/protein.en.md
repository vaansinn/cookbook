Protein builds and repairs muscle, skin, and just about every cell in your body, and it's what keeps a meal feeling filling rather than fleeting. Lentils, chickpeas, eggs, dairy, and fish are the main protein sources across these recipes — combining a grain with a legume (like rice and chickpeas) gives you a fuller amino acid profile than either alone.
