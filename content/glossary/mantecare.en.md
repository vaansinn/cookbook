---
type: technique
title: Mantecare
trigger_words: [mantecare]
---
Italian for "to cream" — finishing a sauce by whisking in cold butter (or sometimes cheese) off the heat, a small piece at a time. The fat emulsifies into the starchy liquid instead of just melting and separating, which is what makes a pasta sauce look glossy and cling to the noodles instead of pooling at the bottom of the bowl.
