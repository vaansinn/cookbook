title: Brunoise
trigger_words: [brunoise]

A brunoise is a very fine, even dice — usually 2-3mm cubes. It matters because uniform pieces cook at the same rate: cut unevenly, some bits turn mushy while others stay raw. Slice the vegetable into thin planks first, stack them, cut into thin strips, then turn the strips 90° and dice.
