---
type: technique
title: Blooming (spices & saffron)
trigger_words: [bloom, blooming, bloomed]
---
Many spices' flavour compounds dissolve in fat or hot liquid, not in the dry powder itself. Blooming means briefly frying ground spices in hot oil, or steeping something like saffron in warm water, before it goes anywhere near the main dish. Skip this step and a spice mostly just tastes dusty; bloom it first and it tastes like itself.
