type: technique
title: Simmering
trigger_words: [simmer, simmering]
---
Simmering means keeping a liquid hot enough for a few small bubbles to rise and break the surface, but not so hot that it's rolling and churning. It sits just below a full boil. Cooking gently like this lets things like lentils or a sauce cook through evenly without falling apart, scorching, or losing all their liquid to steam too fast.
