Carbs are the body's preferred quick-energy source — rice, pasta, and potatoes across these recipes are mostly starch, a carbohydrate that breaks down into glucose during digestion. Whole grains and legumes (like the lentils in the bolognese) also bring fibre alongside the carbs, which slows that breakdown down and keeps energy steadier.
