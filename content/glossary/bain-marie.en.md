---
type: technique
title: Bain-marie (double boiler)
trigger_words: [bain-marie, bain marie, double boiler]
---
A bain-marie is a bowl set over (not touching) a pot of gently simmering water — it heats whatever's in the bowl slowly and evenly with steam instead of direct flame, so eggs cook into a thick sauce or custard instead of scrambling. The bowl's bottom should hover just above the water, never dip into it — direct contact turns gentle heat into a hot spot that can curdle eggs in seconds.
