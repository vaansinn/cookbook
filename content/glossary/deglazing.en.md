---
type: technique
title: Deglazing
trigger_words: [deglaze, deglazing]
---
When food browns in a hot pan, some of it sticks to the bottom as flavourful brown bits (the "fond"). Deglazing means adding liquid — wine, stock, even water — and scraping that fond loose with a wooden spoon while it bubbles. Those bits dissolve back into the liquid and become the base of a deeper-tasting sauce, instead of being wasted or burning.
