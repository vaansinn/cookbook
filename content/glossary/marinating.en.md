---
type: technique
title: Marinating
trigger_words: [marinate, marinating, marinade]
---
A marinade — usually acid (lemon, vinegar, yogurt) plus oil and aromatics — sits meat or vegetables in flavour before they ever hit the heat, and the acid gently breaks down surface proteins so the outer layer turns more tender too. More time isn't always better: a few hours works well, but a strong acid marinade left overnight can turn the surface mushy instead of tender.
