---
type: technique
title: Toasting nuts
trigger_words: [toast the pine nuts, toasted pine nuts, toasting]
---
A few minutes in a dry pan (no oil) over medium heat wakes up a nut's natural oils and turns background crunch into real flavour. They go from pale to burnt fast once they start browning, so shake the pan often and tip them out onto a cold plate the moment they colour — they keep cooking in the pan's residual heat even off the burner.
