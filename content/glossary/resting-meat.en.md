---
type: technique
title: Resting meat
trigger_words: [resting, rest the meat, rest it]
---
Letting cooked meat sit a few minutes off the heat before cutting gives the juices — pushed toward the centre by the heat — time to redistribute through the whole piece instead of running straight out onto the cutting board the second you slice. Skip this step and a perfectly cooked piece of meat can look and taste drier than it needed to.
