---
type: technique
title: Brown butter
trigger_words: [brown butter, browning butter, beurre noisette]
---
Also called beurre noisette ("hazelnut butter") — melt butter and keep cooking it past the foaming stage until the milk solids turn golden-brown and it smells nutty, then take it off the heat immediately before it burns. It turns plain melted butter into something with real depth, and it happens fast — watch it, don't walk away.
