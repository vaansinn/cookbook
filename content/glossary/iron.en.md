Iron carries oxygen through your blood, and plant sources (like the lentils and spinach in these "iron-rich" tagged dishes) are absorbed less efficiently than iron from meat — but pairing them with vitamin C helps close that gap. A squeeze of lemon on a lentil dish, or peppers alongside spinach, meaningfully boosts how much of that iron your body actually takes up.
