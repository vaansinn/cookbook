---
type: technique
title: Creaming butter and sugar
trigger_words: [creaming, creamed]
---
Beating softened butter with sugar until pale and fluffy (2-4 minutes, not 20 seconds) whips tiny air bubbles into the fat. Those bubbles expand in the oven and give cakes and quick breads their lift and soft crumb — skip or rush this step and the result bakes denser and flatter, no matter how good the rest of the recipe is. The butter should be soft enough to dent with a finger but not melted or greasy-looking.
