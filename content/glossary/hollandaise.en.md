---
type: technique
title: Hollandaise (emulsifying with warm butter)
trigger_words: [hollandaise]
---
Hollandaise is an emulsion — warm melted butter whisked into egg yolks so slowly and thoroughly that the fat suspends in tiny droplets instead of pooling. Add the butter too fast and it splits (the sauce separates into greasy puddles instead of staying silky); go slowly, whisking constantly, and the yolks' proteins hold everything together. If it does split, whisking in a teaspoon of cold water off the heat often brings it back.
