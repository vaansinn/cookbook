Fibre is the part of plant food your body can't fully digest — and that's the point. It slows digestion (keeping you fuller for longer), feeds the good bacteria in your gut, and helps everything keep moving. Lentils, chickpeas, and vegetables are the main fibre sources here; the lentil-based dishes in particular carry a lot of it.
