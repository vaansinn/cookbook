type: technique
title: Autolyse
trigger_words: [autolyse]
---
Autolyse means mixing just the flour and water in a bread dough and letting it rest — 20 minutes to a few hours — before adding yeast and salt. The flour hydrates and starts forming gluten on its own during that rest, so the dough needs far less mechanical kneading afterward to develop structure. It's the closest thing bread baking has to "let time do the work instead of your arms."
