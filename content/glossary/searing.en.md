type: technique
title: Searing
trigger_words: [sear, searing]
---
Searing means letting food sit undisturbed in a hot, oiled pan long enough to actually brown, instead of stirring it around. Constant stirring traps steam and the food ends up boiled rather than browned. A real sear needs patience — a few minutes untouched — but it's what builds a crust and real flavour instead of just soft-cooked colour.
