---
type: technique
title: Wok hei
trigger_words: [wok hei]
---
Literally "breath of the wok" — the light, smoky char flavour that comes from stir-frying in a wok that's genuinely hot, ideally hot enough to briefly ignite the oil vapour above the flame. A home stove usually can't get a wok quite that hot, but getting it smoking before anything goes in, and keeping ingredients moving in small batches rather than crowding the pan, gets you most of the way there.
