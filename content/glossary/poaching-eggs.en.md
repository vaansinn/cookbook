type: technique
title: Poaching an egg
trigger_words: [poach, poached, poaching]
---
Poaching cooks an egg gently in barely-simmering water (with a splash of vinegar to help the white hold together), so the white sets while the yolk stays runny. Crack the egg into a small cup first, swirl the water into a gentle whirlpool, and slide it in — the swirl wraps the white neatly around the yolk instead of letting it spread thin.
