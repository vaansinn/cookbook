type: technique
title: Caramelizing onions
trigger_words: [caramelize, caramelizing, caramelized]
---
Caramelizing means cooking onions low and slow — 30 to 45 minutes, stirring occasionally — until their natural sugars break down and brown, turning sweet, jammy, and deep amber instead of just soft. It's different from searing: high heat browns the outside fast while the inside stays sharp and raw-tasting, because the sugars never get the time they need to actually develop.
