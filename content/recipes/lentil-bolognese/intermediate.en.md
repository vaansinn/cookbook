## Prep ahead
- Finely chop the onion, carrot, and celery (the soffritto) before the pan goes on
- Rinse the red lentils
- Put the pasta water on once the sauce is simmering

## Ingredients
- 220|220 g dry spaghetti|pasta_dry
- 150|150 g red lentils, rinsed|lentils_red_dry
- 110|1 onion, finely chopped|onion
- 60|1 carrot, finely chopped|carrot
- 40|1 celery stalk, finely chopped|celery
- 5|1 garlic clove, chopped|garlic
- 400|1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g)|tomatoes_canned
- 16|1 tbsp tomato paste (optional, deepens it)|tomato_paste
- 13.5|1 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 1.5|1 tsp dried oregano or Italian herbs|herbs_dried
- 300|Vegetable bouillon + ~300 ml water|vegetable_bouillon
- 15|Grated parmesan to serve|parmesan

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a pot and soften the onion, carrot, and celery, 6-8 min. Add garlic, 1 min.
2. Stir in the tomato paste, then the lentils, chopped tomatoes, herbs, bouillon and water. Bring to a simmer.
3. Simmer 20-25 min, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft and the sauce thick. Add a splash more water if it gets dry.
4. Meanwhile, boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Scoop out some pasta water before draining.
5. Toss the pasta with the sauce, loosening with a splash of pasta water. Taste, adjust salt, serve with parmesan.

## Notes
- Red lentils melt into the sauce; brown or green lentils stay firmer if you prefer texture.
- Great with a pinch of chili or a splash of red wine in step 2.
- Equipment: one pot (sauce) + one pot (pasta).
