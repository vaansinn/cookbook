## Prep ahead
- Rinse the red lentils
- Put the pasta water on to boil

## Ingredients
- 220|220 g dry spaghetti|pasta_dry
- 150|150 g red lentils, rinsed|lentils_red_dry
- 450|1 jar tomato pasta sauce (~450 g)|tomato_pasta_sauce_jar
- 5|1 tsp dried Italian herbs|herbs_dried
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Rinse the lentils, then simmer them in a pot with double their volume of water for 12-15 min until soft. Drain.
2. Meanwhile, boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
3. Heat the jar sauce in a wide pan with the herbs, 3-4 min. Stir in the cooked lentils.
4. Drain the pasta and toss it straight into the sauce. Serve.

## Notes
- The jar does the seasoning work here — taste before adding extra salt.
- Equipment: one pot (lentils + pasta, one after the other) + one pan for the sauce.
