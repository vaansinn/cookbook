## Prep ahead
- Finely dice the onion, tomato, and jalapeño before you touch the avocados — cut avocado browns fast, so it should be the last thing you handle
- Check the avocados are ripe

## Ingredients
- 480|3 avocados, halved and pitted|avocado
- 30|Juice of 1 lime|lime
- 50|1/4 onion, finely diced|onion
- 100|1 tomato, seeded and diced|tomato_fresh
- 10|1 tbsp cilantro, chopped|coriander_fresh
- 5|1 garlic clove, minced|garlic
- 15|1/2 jalapeño, finely diced (seeds removed for less heat)|jalapeno_fresh
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl and mash to your preferred chunkiness — some smooth, some chunky is the classic texture.
2. Fold in the onion, tomato, cilantro, garlic, and jalapeño.
3. Add the lime juice and salt, taste, and adjust — guacamole should taste bright, not flat.

## Notes
- Seeding the tomato (scooping out the watery core) keeps the guacamole from turning soupy.
- Equipment: one bowl.
