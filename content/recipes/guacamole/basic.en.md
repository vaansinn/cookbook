## Prep ahead
- Check the avocados are ripe — they should give slightly when pressed, not feel hard or mushy

## Ingredients
- 480|3 avocados, halved and pitted|avocado
- 30|Juice of 1 lime|lime
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl.
2. Mash with a fork to your preferred chunkiness.
3. Stir in the lime juice and salt. Taste and adjust. Serve with tortilla chips.

## Notes
- Three ingredients, no chopping — this is the whole recipe.
- Equipment: one bowl.
