## Prep ahead
- Start the rice first — it cooks unattended while the curry comes together
- Drain and cube the tofu

## Ingredients
- 250|250 g rice|rice_dry
- 60|4 tbsp green curry paste|curry_paste_green_jarred
- 400|1 can (400 ml) coconut milk|coconut_milk
- 300|300 g firm tofu, cubed|tofu_firm
- 120|1 bell pepper, sliced|bell_pepper
- 18|1 tbsp fish sauce|fish_sauce
- 6|1 tsp sugar|sugar

## Instructions
1. Cook the rice: rinse, then simmer covered in ~1.5x its volume of water for 10-12 min, rest 5 min off the heat.
2. Heat the curry paste in a pot over medium heat, stirring, 1 min.
3. Add the coconut milk, tofu, and bell pepper. Simmer 10 min until the vegetables are tender.
4. Stir in the fish sauce and sugar. Taste and adjust. Serve over rice.

## Notes
- Jarred curry paste does the heavy lifting here — check the label, some are spicier than others.
- Equipment: one pot.
