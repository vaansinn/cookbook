## Prep ahead
- Don't shake the coconut milk can — let it sit so the thick cream separates and rises to the top
- Start the rice first
- Pick the Thai basil leaves and have the lime ready to cut

## Ingredients
- 250|250 g rice|rice_dry
- 400|1 can (400 ml) coconut milk, cream separated|coconut_milk
- 60|4 tbsp green curry paste|curry_paste_green_jarred
- 300|300 g firm tofu, cubed|tofu_firm
- 120|1 bell pepper, sliced|bell_pepper
- 18|1 tbsp fish sauce|fish_sauce
- 6|1 tsp sugar|sugar
- 8|A handful of Thai basil leaves|thai_basil
- 8|1/2 lime, cut into wedges|lime

## Instructions
1. Cook the rice: rinse, then simmer covered in ~1.5x its volume of water for 10-12 min, rest 5 min off the heat.
2. Scoop the thick coconut cream off the top of the can into a pot. Heat it over medium heat until it starts to sizzle and separate slightly, then add the curry paste and fry it in the cream, stirring, 2-3 min until fragrant — this blooms the paste properly instead of just simmering it raw.
3. Add the tofu and bell pepper, stirring to coat in the paste. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk and simmer 8-10 min.
4. Stir in the fish sauce and sugar. Take off the heat, then fold in the Thai basil so it wilts from residual heat rather than cooking dull. Serve over rice with lime wedges.

## Notes
- Frying the paste in just the coconut cream (not the whole can at once) is what makes a curry taste layered instead of just "coconut milk with paste stirred in."
- Add the basil off the heat — cooked too long, it turns dark and loses its bright anise flavour.
- Equipment: one pot.
