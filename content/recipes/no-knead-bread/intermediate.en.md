## Prep ahead
- This version uses more yeast for a same-day rise — mix it in the morning for bread by evening
- Have a lame, razor, or very sharp knife ready for scoring right before baking

## Ingredients
- 400|3 cups flour|flour_wheat
- 7|1 tsp instant yeast|yeast_dry
- 12|2 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Stir the flour, yeast, and salt together. Add ~350 ml water and mix with a spoon until no dry flour remains — shaggy and wet, not kneaded.
2. Cover and leave at room temperature 3-4 hours, until roughly doubled and bubbly on top.
3. Turn the dough onto a floured surface. Shape it properly this time: fold the edges into the middle from all sides, then flip seam-side down and cup your hands around it, dragging it slightly on the counter to build surface tension into a taut ball.
4. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F) with the empty covered pot inside for 30 min. Score the top of the dough with one confident slash, about 1 cm deep.
5. Carefully drop the dough into the hot pot (scored-side up), cover, and bake 30 min. Remove the lid, bake 15-20 min more until deep golden. Cool on a rack at least 20 min.

## Notes
- Proper shaping (building tension into the ball) is what makes a loaf rise up rather than spread out — a loose, untensioned shape bakes flatter.
- Scoring isn't decorative — it gives the crust a controlled place to expand, instead of splitting randomly.
- Equipment: one bowl (mixing) + one heavy lidded pot (baking) + a sharp blade (scoring).
