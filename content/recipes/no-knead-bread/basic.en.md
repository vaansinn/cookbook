## Prep ahead
- This bread needs 12-18 hours of unattended rising — mix it the night before you want fresh bread
- Make sure your pot (a Dutch oven or any heavy lidded pot) can go in a very hot oven

## Ingredients
- 400|3 cups flour|flour_wheat
- 2.3|1/4 tsp instant yeast|yeast_dry
- 12|2 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Stir the flour, yeast, and salt together in a bowl. Add ~350 ml water and stir with a spoon until no dry flour remains — it will look shaggy and wet, not like a normal dough. Don't knead it.
2. Cover the bowl and leave at room temperature for 12-18 hours, until the surface is dotted with bubbles.
3. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F) with the empty covered pot inside, for 30 min.
4. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface, fold it over on itself a couple of times, and shape roughly into a ball. Carefully drop it into the hot pot, cover, and bake 30 min.
5. Remove the lid and bake another 15-20 min until deep golden brown. Cool on a rack at least 20 min before slicing.

## Notes
- The long, slow rise does the work that kneading would normally do — this is the easiest real bread there is, it just needs patience, not skill.
- Equipment: one bowl (mixing) + one heavy lidded pot (baking).
