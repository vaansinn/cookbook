## Prep ahead
- None — this comes together as fast as you can chop

## Ingredients
- 200|1 cucumber, chopped|cucumber
- 200|2 tomatoes, chopped|tomato_fresh
- 100|100 g feta, cubed|feta
- 60|A handful of Kalamata olives|olives_kalamata
- 27|2 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Toss the cucumber, tomatoes, feta, and olives together in a bowl.
2. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt, and serve.

## Notes
- No dressing to make — the olive oil and the vegetables' own juices are the dressing.
- Equipment: one bowl.
