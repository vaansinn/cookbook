## Prep ahead
- Salt the cucumber and tomato and let them sit 15 min before assembling — this is the step that keeps the salad from turning watery

## Ingredients
- 200|1 cucumber, chopped|cucumber
- 200|2 tomatoes, chopped|tomato_fresh
- 3|1/2 tsp salt (for drawing out water)|salt
- 60|1/4 red onion, thinly sliced|onion
- 60|A handful of Kalamata olives|olives_kalamata
- 100|100 g feta, in one slab|feta
- 27|2 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 1.3|1/2 tsp dried oregano|herbs_dried

## Instructions
1. Toss the cucumber and tomato with the salt in a colander and let them sit 15 min to release excess water. Don't rinse — just let it drain.
2. Combine the drained cucumber and tomato with the onion and olives in a bowl.
3. Lay the block of feta on top rather than crumbling it — this is the traditional presentation, and it keeps the cheese from disappearing into the vegetables.
4. Drizzle with olive oil and scatter oregano over the top. Serve.

## Notes
- Salting and draining first is the difference between a crisp salad and a puddle at the bottom of the bowl.
- Equipment: one bowl + a colander.
