## Prep ahead
- Salt the cucumber and tomato early — they need the full 20-30 min to drain properly
- Marinate the feta and olives in oil and herbs while the vegetables drain, so everything's had time to pick up flavour by the time it's assembled

## Ingredients
- 200|1 cucumber, chopped|cucumber
- 200|2 tomatoes, chopped|tomato_fresh
- 3|1/2 tsp salt (for drawing out water)|salt
- 100|100 g feta, in one slab|feta
- 60|A handful of Kalamata olives|olives_kalamata
- 40|3 tbsp olive oil, divided|olive_oil
- 1.3|1/2 tsp dried oregano, divided|herbs_dried
- 60|1/4 red onion, very thinly sliced|onion
- 15|1/2 lemon, juiced|lemon

## Instructions
1. Toss the cucumber and tomato with the salt in a colander. Let drain 20-30 min, tossing once halfway.
2. Meanwhile, marinate: pour half the olive oil and half the oregano over the feta slab and olives in a small dish. Let sit at room temperature while everything else comes together — this is the same principle as marinating meat, just for cheese and olives instead.
3. In a jar, shake the remaining olive oil with the lemon juice until it emulsifies into a light dressing.
4. Combine the drained cucumber and tomato with the raw red onion in a bowl. Toss with the dressing.
5. Top with the marinated feta slab and olives, drizzling over any oil left in the marinating dish. Scatter the remaining oregano.

## Notes
- Marinating the feta and olives even briefly gives them a rounder flavour than adding them straight from the fridge.
- Very thinly sliced raw onion (rather than chunks) softens its bite without needing to cook it.
- Equipment: one bowl + a colander + a small jar (for the dressing).
