## Prep ahead
- Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease a loaf tin
- The riper and more spotty the bananas, the sweeter and better this turns out

## Ingredients
- 300|3 ripe bananas, mashed|banana
- 80|1/3 cup oil|olive_oil
- 150|3/4 cup sugar|sugar
- 100|2 eggs|eggs
- 240|2 cups flour|flour_wheat
- 5|1 tsp baking soda|baking_soda
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Mash the bananas in a large bowl. Stir in the oil, sugar, and eggs until combined.
2. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir just until no dry streaks remain — a few lumps are fine.
3. Pour into the greased tin. Bake 55-60 min, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
4. Cool in the tin 10 min, then turn out onto a rack to cool fully before slicing.

## Notes
- One bowl, one spoon — no mixer needed for this version.
- Equipment: one bowl + one loaf tin.
