## Prep ahead
- Take the butter out early — it needs to be genuinely soft for creaming, not melted
- Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease and line a loaf tin
- Mash the bananas separately so they're ready to fold in last

## Ingredients
- 113|1/2 cup (113 g) butter, softened|butter
- 150|3/4 cup sugar|sugar
- 100|2 eggs|eggs
- 300|3 ripe bananas, mashed|banana
- 4.2|1 tsp vanilla extract|vanilla_extract
- 240|2 cups flour|flour_wheat
- 5|1 tsp baking soda|baking_soda
- 2.6|1 tsp cinnamon|cinnamon_ground
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Cream the softened butter and sugar together until pale and fluffy, 2-3 min with a hand mixer or by hand with a wooden spoon.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla. Fold in the mashed bananas.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet in two additions, mixing just until combined — overmixing here is what makes banana bread tough instead of tender.
4. Pour into the tin. Bake 55-60 min until a toothpick comes out clean, tenting with foil after 40 min if the top is browning too fast.
5. Cool in the tin 10 min, then turn out onto a rack to cool fully.

## Notes
- Creaming the butter and sugar properly is what gives this version a noticeably softer, lighter crumb than the oil-based basic version.
- A loose foil tent partway through baking stops the top from over-browning before the centre is done.
- Equipment: one mixing bowl (or two, for wet/dry) + one loaf tin.
