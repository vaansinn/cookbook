## Prep ahead
- Brown the butter first and let it cool back to a soft, spreadable state before creaming — a hot browned butter will melt the sugar structure instead of creaming into it
- Toast the walnuts and let them cool while the butter browns
- Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease and line a loaf tin

## Ingredients
- 113|1/2 cup (113 g) butter, browned and cooled to soft|butter
- 150|3/4 cup sugar|sugar
- 100|2 eggs|eggs
- 300|3 very ripe bananas, mashed|banana
- 4.2|1 tsp vanilla extract|vanilla_extract
- 240|2 cups flour|flour_wheat
- 5|1 tsp baking soda|baking_soda
- 2.6|1 tsp cinnamon|cinnamon_ground
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 60|1/2 cup walnuts, toasted and roughly chopped|walnuts

## Instructions
1. Brown the butter: melt it in a pan over medium heat, swirling often, until the milk solids turn golden-brown and it smells nutty. Pour into a bowl and let cool until soft and spreadable again, about 20 min — it must not be liquid or hot when you cream it.
2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan, 2-3 min, shaking often, then chop once cool.
3. Cream the cooled brown butter with the sugar until pale and fluffy, 2-3 min. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, then fold in the mashed bananas.
4. Whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a separate bowl. Fold into the wet ingredients in two additions, just until combined, then fold in the walnuts.
5. Pour into the tin. Bake 55-65 min, tenting with foil after 40 min. Test with a toothpick in the very centre — it should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter, but not bone dry either.
6. Cool in the tin 10 min, then turn out onto a rack to cool fully before slicing.

## Notes
- Browning the butter trades a little of the creaming step's airiness for a deep, nutty flavour that plain butter can't give — worth the extra 10 minutes.
- A toothpick with a few moist crumbs (not wet batter, not totally clean) is the real doneness test — bake until totally clean and the loaf dries out.
- Equipment: one small pan (browning butter, toasting walnuts) + one mixing bowl + one loaf tin.
