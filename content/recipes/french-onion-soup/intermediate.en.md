## Prep ahead
- Slice the onions evenly so they cook at the same rate
- Have the wine measured out — it goes in partway through the long cook

## Ingredients
- 440|4 onions, sliced|onion
- 28|2 tbsp butter|butter
- 60|60 ml white wine|white_wine
- 500|500 ml beef stock|beef_stock
- 1.5|1/2 tsp dried thyme|herbs_dried
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 60|2 slices baguette|baguette
- 80|80 g Gruyère, grated|cheese_gruyere

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium-low heat. Add the onions and cook low and slow, stirring every few minutes, 40-45 min, until deeply golden and jammy — resist the urge to raise the heat to speed this up.
2. Pour in the wine, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot. Simmer 2 min to cook off the sharp alcohol edge.
3. Add the stock and thyme, season with salt, and simmer 15 min.
4. Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top with baguette and Gruyère, and broil 3-5 min until bubbling and browned.

## Notes
- Deglazing with wine partway through the cook picks up the fond (the browned bits stuck to the pot) that would otherwise be wasted flavour.
- Equipment: one pot + oven-safe bowls.
