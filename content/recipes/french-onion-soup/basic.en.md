## Prep ahead
- Slice all the onions before the pan goes on — once they're cooking you won't want to stop and chop

## Ingredients
- 440|4 onions, sliced|onion
- 28|2 tbsp butter|butter
- 500|500 ml beef stock|beef_stock
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 60|2 slices baguette|baguette
- 60|60 g Gruyère, grated|cheese_gruyere

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium-high heat. Add the onions and cook, stirring often, 20-25 min until soft and golden.
2. Pour in the stock, add salt, and simmer 10 min.
3. Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top each with a slice of baguette and a handful of Gruyère.
4. Broil 3-5 min until the cheese is bubbling and browned. Serve hot.

## Notes
- This is the quicker version — real caramelization takes longer, but this still gets you a good, fast bowl of onion soup.
- Equipment: one pot + oven-safe bowls.
