## Prep ahead
- Melt the butter before you start the blender hollandaise — it needs to go in hot
- Have the muffins ready to toast and the ham ready to warm so all three components finish close together
- Juice the lemon

## Ingredients
- 114|2 English muffins, split|english_muffin
- 100|4 slices ham|ham
- 200|4 eggs, for poaching|eggs
- 15|1 tbsp white vinegar|vinegar_white
- 34|2 egg yolks, for the hollandaise|egg_yolk
- 113|1/2 cup (113 g) butter, melted and hot|butter
- 8|1/2 lemon, juiced|lemon
- 0.9|A pinch of cayenne pepper|cayenne_pepper
- 3|A pinch of chives, chopped|chives_fresh

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter until hot and bubbling but not browned.
2. Blend the egg yolks, lemon juice, and cayenne for 20 sec until pale. With the blender running, drizzle in the hot butter in a thin, steady stream — this is what emulsifies it into hollandaise instead of scrambling the yolks. Stop as soon as it thickens.
3. Toast the muffins and warm the ham while the hollandaise rests.
4. Bring water to a bare simmer, add the vinegar. Poach the eggs 3 min, one at a time, sliding each into a gentle whirlpool.
5. Stack muffin, ham, poached egg, hollandaise. Scatter chives, serve immediately — hollandaise doesn't reheat well.

## Notes
- The blender method is the fast, forgiving way to make real hollandaise — the motor does the whisking a traditional recipe asks your arm to do.
- If the sauce is too thick, blend in a teaspoon of warm water.
- Equipment: one pot (poaching) + one pan (ham) + a blender (hollandaise).
