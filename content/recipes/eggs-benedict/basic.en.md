## Prep ahead
- Toast the English muffins just before serving — they need to still be warm
- Warm the ham in a dry pan while the eggs poach

## Ingredients
- 114|2 English muffins, split|english_muffin
- 100|4 slices ham|ham
- 200|4 eggs, for poaching|eggs
- 15|1 tbsp white vinegar (for the poaching water)|vinegar_white
- 150|1 jar prepared hollandaise sauce, warmed|hollandaise_jarred
- 3|A pinch of chives, chopped (optional)|chives_fresh

## Instructions
1. Toast the muffin halves. Warm the ham slices in a dry pan, 1-2 min per side.
2. Bring a pot of water to a bare simmer, add the vinegar. Crack each egg into a small cup, swirl the water, and slide the egg in. Poach 3 min for a runny yolk.
3. Warm the hollandaise in a small pan or the microwave, stirring often so it doesn't split.
4. Stack: muffin half, ham, poached egg, a spoon of hollandaise. Scatter chives. Serve immediately.

## Notes
- The jar sauce skips the trickiest part of this dish entirely — just don't let it boil, or it can split.
- Equipment: one pot (poaching) + one pan (ham).
