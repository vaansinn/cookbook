## Prep ahead
- The marinade needs at least 2 hours; the tzatziki improves if made at the same time so its flavours have time to meld too
- Grate the cucumber for the tzatziki and squeeze it dry — skipping this waters down the sauce
- Let the chicken sit at room temperature 15 min before grilling so it cooks evenly

## Ingredients
- 300|300 g chicken breast, cubed|chicken_breast
- 75|75 g plain yogurt, for the marinade|yogurt_plain
- 13.5|1 tbsp olive oil, for the marinade|olive_oil
- 15|1/2 lemon, juiced, for the marinade|lemon
- 10|2 garlic cloves, grated, for the marinade|garlic
- 1.3|1/2 tsp dried oregano|herbs_dried
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 150|150 g plain yogurt, for the tzatziki|yogurt_plain
- 100|1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry|cucumber
- 5|1 garlic clove, grated, for the tzatziki|garlic
- 13.5|1 tbsp olive oil, for the tzatziki|olive_oil
- 128|2 pita breads|pita_bread

## Instructions
1. Mix the marinade ingredients in a bowl, add the chicken, and marinate in the fridge 2-3 hours.
2. Make the tzatziki: stir the grated, squeezed cucumber into the second batch of yogurt with the garlic and olive oil. Season with salt, cover, and chill — it's better after an hour of resting.
3. Thread the marinated chicken onto skewers, shaking off excess marinade.
4. Get the grill or grill pan properly hot before the chicken goes on — you want an immediate sizzle, not a slow warm-up, for real charring instead of grey, steamed chicken.
5. Grill 10-12 min, turning every 2-3 min, until charred in spots and cooked through.
6. Rest the skewers 5 min off the heat before serving — this lets the juices settle back through the meat instead of running out the moment you bite in.
7. Warm the pita and serve with the tzatziki alongside.

## Notes
- A properly hot grill is what turns "cooked chicken" into "grilled chicken" — a lukewarm pan just poaches it in its own juices.
- Resting isn't optional here — cubed chicken loses juice fast when cut into straight off the grill.
- Equipment: two bowls (marinade, tzatziki) + skewers + a grill or grill pan.
