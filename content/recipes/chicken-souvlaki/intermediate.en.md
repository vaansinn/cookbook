## Prep ahead
- The yogurt marinade needs 2 hours minimum — start this well ahead
- Soak wooden skewers 20 min if using them

## Ingredients
- 300|300 g chicken breast, cubed|chicken_breast
- 75|75 g plain yogurt|yogurt_plain
- 13.5|1 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 15|1/2 lemon, juiced|lemon
- 5|1 garlic clove, grated|garlic
- 1.3|1/2 tsp dried oregano|herbs_dried
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 128|2 pita breads|pita_bread

## Instructions
1. Mix the yogurt, oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and salt in a bowl. Add the chicken and turn to coat.
2. Cover and marinate in the fridge at least 2 hours, up to 8. The yogurt tenderizes the chicken while the acid and garlic season it through, not just on the surface.
3. Thread the chicken onto skewers, letting excess marinade drip off first — too much on the surface just steams instead of charring.
4. Grill over medium-high heat, turning every 2-3 min, 10-12 min until cooked through with good charring on a few sides.
5. Warm the pita and serve.

## Notes
- Yogurt-based marinades are common across Mediterranean and Middle Eastern grilling for a reason — the mild acid tenderizes without the mushiness a stronger acid marinade can cause.
- Equipment: one bowl + skewers + a grill or grill pan.
