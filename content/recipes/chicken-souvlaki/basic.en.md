## Prep ahead
- Cut the chicken into even cubes so they cook at the same rate
- If using wooden skewers, soak them in water 20 min so they don't burn

## Ingredients
- 300|300 g chicken breast, cubed|chicken_breast
- 27|2 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 15|1/2 lemon, juiced|lemon
- 1.3|1/2 tsp dried oregano|herbs_dried
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 128|2 pita breads|pita_bread

## Instructions
1. Toss the chicken with the oil, lemon juice, oregano, and salt. Let sit 20 min while the grill or grill pan heats.
2. Thread the chicken onto skewers.
3. Grill over medium-high heat, turning every 2-3 min, 10-12 min total until cooked through and lightly charred.
4. Warm the pita and serve the skewers alongside.

## Notes
- A grill pan on the stove works just as well as an outdoor grill for this.
- Equipment: one bowl + skewers + a grill or grill pan.
