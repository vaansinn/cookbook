## Prep ahead
- Dice the potatoes small so they cook through quickly
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas

## Ingredients
- 500|500 g potatoes, diced small|potatoes
- 240|1 can chickpeas (400 g), drained|chickpeas_canned
- 400|1 jar tomato pasta sauce|tomato_pasta_sauce_jar
- 2.3|1 tsp smoked paprika|smoked_paprika
- 27|2 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 100|100 g feta, crumbled|feta
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet and fry the diced potatoes over medium heat until starting to brown and nearly tender, ~12-15 min.
2. Stir in the smoked paprika, 30 sec until fragrant.
3. Add the chickpeas and jar sauce. Simmer 8-10 min until the potatoes are fully tender.
4. Crumble the feta on top and serve straight from the pan.

## Notes
- One jar, one pan — no chopping onions or garlic.
- Equipment: one large skillet.
