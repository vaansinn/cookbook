## Prep ahead
- Put the pasta water on to boil first
- Take the ricotta out of the fridge early so it mixes smoothly

## Ingredients
- 190|190 g dry spaghettini|pasta_dry
- 250|250 g ricotta|ricotta
- 15|Juice of 1 lemon (bottled is fine)|lemon
- 15|30 g grated parmesan|parmesan
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 1.15|0.5 tsp black pepper|black_pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil the spaghettini in salted water until al dente. Scoop out ~250 ml pasta water before draining.
2. Mix the ricotta with the lemon juice, parmesan, salt and pepper until smooth.
3. Return the pasta to the pot, add the ricotta mixture, and toss, adding pasta water a splash at a time until it's saucy. Serve.

## Notes
- No garlic, no spinach, no zesting — just mix and toss.
- Equipment: one pot.
