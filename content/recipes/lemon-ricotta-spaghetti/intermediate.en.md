## Prep ahead
- Put the pasta water on to boil first — everything else fits inside the pasta's cooking time
- Take the ricotta out of the fridge early so it mixes smoothly
- Zest and juice the lemon and chop the garlic before the pasta goes in
- Set a mug by the pot as a reminder to save pasta water before draining

## Ingredients
- 190|190 g dry spaghettini (n.3)|pasta_dry
- 250|250 g ricotta|ricotta
- 15|1 lemon, zested and juiced|lemon
- 150|150 g fresh spinach|spinach_fresh
- 5|1 garlic clove, finely chopped|garlic
- 13.5|1 tbsp olive oil|olive_oil
- 15|30 g grated parmesan (plus extra to serve)|parmesan
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 1.15|0.5 tsp black pepper|black_pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil the spaghettini in salted water until al dente. Scoop out ~250 ml pasta water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, mix the ricotta with the lemon zest, lemon juice, parmesan, salt and pepper until smooth. Start with half the juice and adjust at the end — ricotta brands vary in tang.
3. Heat the olive oil in the emptied pasta pot, briefly sizzle the garlic without browning, then wilt the spinach, ~2 min.
4. Return the pasta, add the ricotta mixture, and toss over low heat, adding pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce is silky. Taste, adjust salt and lemon, serve with extra parmesan and pepper.

## Notes
- The starchy pasta water is what turns the ricotta into a proper sauce — don't skip it.
- Extra protein: white beans or toasted pine nuts. A pinch of chili flakes works well.
- Equipment: one pot (reused for the spinach) + one mixing bowl.
