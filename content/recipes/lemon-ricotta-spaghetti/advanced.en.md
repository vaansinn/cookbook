## Prep ahead
- Toast the pine nuts before anything else goes on the stove — they burn fast and need your full attention for 2 minutes
- Zest the lemon before juicing it — you can't get zest back once it's juiced
- Have the butter cold and cubed, ready to go in off the heat

## Ingredients
- 190|190 g dry spaghettini|pasta_dry
- 250|250 g ricotta|ricotta
- 15|1 lemon, zested and juiced|lemon
- 150|150 g fresh spinach|spinach_fresh
- 5|1 garlic clove, finely chopped|garlic
- 20|20 g butter, cold, cubed|butter
- 20|20 g pine nuts|pine_nuts
- 20|40 g grated parmesan (plus extra to serve)|parmesan
- 8|A few basil leaves, torn|basil_fresh
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt
- 1.15|0.5 tsp black pepper, cracked|black_pepper

## Instructions
1. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking often, 2-3 min until golden. Tip out immediately — they keep browning in the residual heat of the pan.
2. Boil the spaghettini in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve a full cup of pasta water.
3. Meanwhile, mix the ricotta with the lemon zest, half the juice, parmesan, salt and pepper until smooth.
4. In the emptied pasta pot, melt half the butter and briefly sizzle the garlic without browning it, then wilt the spinach, ~2 min.
5. Add the pasta and ricotta mixture off the heat, tossing and adding pasta water gradually until the sauce clings rather than pools — this is the same emulsion technique as a carbonara, just built around ricotta instead of egg.
6. Whisk in the remaining cold butter cube by cube (this is what makes it glossy). Taste, adjust with the remaining lemon juice if it needs brightness.
7. Finish with the toasted pine nuts, torn basil, and extra parmesan.

## Notes
- Cold butter whisked in off the heat (mantecare) is what turns a good sauce silky instead of just wet — the fat emulsifies into the starchy pasta water instead of separating.
- Toast the pine nuts before you start anything else — a burnt batch means starting over, and it happens in seconds once they turn.
- Equipment: one pot (pasta, reused for the sauce) + one small dry pan (pine nuts).
