## Prep ahead
- Start the rice first — it cooks unattended while the sauce comes together
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas
- Chop the onion and grate the garlic and ginger before the pan goes on

## Ingredients
- 250|250 g rice (basmati or long grain)|rice_dry
- 480|2 cans chickpeas a 400 g, drained|chickpeas_canned
- 450|1 jar tikka masala sauce (~450 g)|tikka_masala_sauce
- 110|1 onion, chopped|onion
- 5|1 garlic clove, grated|garlic
- 15|1 small piece ginger, grated|ginger_fresh
- 13.5|1 tbsp oil|olive_oil
- 50|Handful spinach (optional, for iron + colour)|spinach_fresh
- 10|Fresh coriander or parsley to serve (optional)|coriander_fresh

## Instructions
1. Cook the rice: rinse, then simmer covered in ~1.5x its volume of water for 10-12 min, rest 5 min off the heat.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a wide pan and soften the onion, 5 min. Add garlic and ginger, 1 min more.
3. Add the drained chickpeas and stir to coat, 2 min.
4. Pour in the jar of tikka masala sauce. Rinse the jar with a small splash of water and add that too. Simmer gently 8-10 min.
5. Stir in the spinach at the end until wilted. Taste before adding any salt — the jar is already seasoned. Serve over rice with herbs on top.

## Notes
- Ready-sauce version: no extra cream or tomatoes needed, the jar covers both.
- Swap chickpeas for 2 chicken breasts (browned in step 3) for a meat version.
- One jar is right for two generous portions; add the second can of chickpeas only if you like it very chunky.
- Equipment: one pot (rice) + one wide pan.
