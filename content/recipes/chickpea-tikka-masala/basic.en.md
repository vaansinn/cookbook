## Prep ahead
- Start the rice first — it cooks unattended while everything else happens
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas

## Ingredients
- 250|250 g rice (basmati or long grain)|rice_dry
- 480|2 cans chickpeas a 400 g, drained|chickpeas_canned
- 450|1 jar tikka masala sauce (~450 g)|tikka_masala_sauce
- 6|1 tsp salt|salt

## Instructions
1. Cook the rice: rinse, then simmer covered in ~1.5x its volume of water for 10-12 min, rest 5 min off the heat.
2. Meanwhile, heat the jar sauce in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add the drained chickpeas and simmer 8-10 min until hot through. Taste before salting — the jar is already seasoned.
4. Serve over the rice.

## Notes
- No chopping, no spices to measure — the jar is doing the work here.
- Equipment: one pot (rice) + one pan (sauce).
