## Prep ahead
- Make the coffee and let it cool to room temperature before dipping — hot coffee turns the biscuits to mush
- Leave the mascarpone out to soften for 15 min so it mixes smoothly

## Ingredients
- 250|250 g mascarpone|mascarpone
- 50|50 g sugar|sugar
- 200|200 ml strong brewed coffee, cooled|coffee_brewed
- 200|200 g ladyfinger biscuits|ladyfingers
- 10|2 tbsp cocoa powder, for dusting|cocoa_powder

## Instructions
1. Beat the mascarpone and sugar together until smooth.
2. Pour the coffee into a shallow dish. Dip each ladyfinger for about 1 second per side — just enough to soak in, not so long it falls apart — and layer them in a dish.
3. Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the biscuits. Repeat with a second layer of dipped biscuits and the rest of the mascarpone.
4. Dust generously with cocoa powder. Chill at least 2 hours before serving.

## Notes
- No raw eggs and no double boiler — this version is all about the coffee-soaked biscuit layers.
- Equipment: one bowl (mascarpone) + one dish (assembly).
