## Prep ahead
- Separate the eggs carefully — even a speck of yolk in the whites stops them whipping properly
- Make the coffee and let it cool to room temperature
- Have the mascarpone at room temperature so it folds in smoothly

## Ingredients
- 68|4 egg yolks|egg_yolk
- 100|100 g sugar|sugar
- 250|250 g mascarpone|mascarpone
- 132|4 egg whites|egg_white
- 200|200 ml strong brewed coffee, cooled|coffee_brewed
- 200|200 g ladyfinger biscuits|ladyfingers
- 10|2 tbsp cocoa powder, for dusting|cocoa_powder

## Instructions
1. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar together until pale, thick, and doubled in volume, 3-4 min. Fold in the mascarpone until smooth.
2. In a clean, dry bowl, whisk the egg whites to soft peaks. Fold them into the mascarpone mixture in two additions — gently, cutting through the middle and folding over, to keep the air in rather than knocking it out.
3. Dip the ladyfingers briefly in the coffee, 1 second per side, and layer half of them in a dish.
4. Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the top. Repeat with the remaining biscuits and mixture.
5. Dust with cocoa powder. Chill at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.

## Notes
- Whipped egg whites folded in (rather than just whipped cream) is what gives real tiramisu its light, mousse-like texture instead of a dense one.
- Fold, don't stir — stirring collapses the air you just whipped in.
- Equipment: two bowls (yolks + whites) + one dish (assembly).
