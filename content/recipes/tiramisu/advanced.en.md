## Prep ahead
- Set up the bain-marie before you start whisking — the zabaglione needs constant attention once it begins
- Brew the coffee strong and let it cool; stir the marsala into it once cool, not while hot (alcohol cooks off in heat)
- Separate the eggs; have the whites in a spotlessly clean, dry bowl

## Ingredients
- 68|4 egg yolks|egg_yolk
- 100|100 g sugar|sugar
- 60|60 ml marsala wine|marsala_wine
- 250|250 g mascarpone|mascarpone
- 132|4 egg whites|egg_white
- 180|180 ml strong brewed coffee, cooled|coffee_brewed
- 30|2 tbsp marsala wine, for the coffee dip|marsala_wine
- 200|200 g ladyfinger biscuits|ladyfingers
- 10|2 tbsp cocoa powder, for dusting|cocoa_powder

## Instructions
1. Make the zabaglione: whisk the egg yolks, sugar, and 60 ml marsala together in a heatproof bowl. Set over a bain-marie of barely simmering water (bowl not touching the water) and whisk constantly, 8-10 min, until pale, thick, and roughly tripled in volume — it should hold a ribbon trail when you lift the whisk. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly.
2. Fold the mascarpone into the cooled zabaglione until smooth and no streaks remain.
3. Whisk the egg whites in a clean bowl to soft, glossy peaks. Fold into the mascarpone mixture in two additions, gently.
4. Stir the extra marsala into the cooled coffee. Dip the ladyfingers briefly, 1 second per side, and layer half in a dish.
5. Spread half the cream over the biscuits. Repeat with the remaining biscuits and cream.
6. Dust with cocoa powder just before serving (any earlier and it soaks in). Chill at least 6 hours, ideally overnight — the flavours need time to meld.

## Notes
- Zabaglione (egg yolks + sugar + marsala, cooked gently over a bain-marie) is the traditional base of tiramisu — it's what separates the classic version from the simplified raw-yolk approach.
- Watch the bain-marie heat closely: if the water boils hard, the eggs can scramble in seconds. Lift the bowl off if it looks like it's going too fast.
- Equipment: a heatproof bowl (zabaglione) + two other bowls (whites, coffee dip) + one dish (assembly).
