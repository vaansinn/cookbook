## Prep ahead
- Best with cold, day-old rice — fresh warm rice goes mushy
- Beat the eggs before you start — frying goes fast

## Ingredients
- 400|~400 g cooked cold rice|rice_cooked
- 150|3 eggs, beaten|eggs
- 100|100 g spinach, roughly chopped|spinach_fresh
- 32|2 tbsp soy sauce|soy_sauce
- 13.5|1 tbsp oil|olive_oil

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Pour in the beaten eggs, scramble softly, then set aside.
2. Add the cold rice, breaking up clumps, and fry 3-4 min until hot and slightly toasted.
3. Stir in the spinach until wilted, return the egg, add soy sauce and toss. Serve.

## Notes
- No onion, no garlic, no extras — the clean-out-the-fridge version.
- Equipment: one wok or large pan.
