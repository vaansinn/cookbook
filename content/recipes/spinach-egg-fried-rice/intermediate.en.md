## Prep ahead
- Best with cold, day-old rice — cook it the night before (or from dinner 1) and chill it
- Beat the eggs, chop the spinach and any veg before you start — frying goes fast
- Have soy sauce and everything within reach; the wok waits for no one

## Ingredients
- 400|~400 g cooked cold rice (from ~200 g dry)|rice_cooked
- 150|3 eggs, beaten|eggs
- 100|100 g spinach, roughly chopped|spinach_fresh
- 120|1 red bell pepper, diced (optional)|bell_pepper
- 30|2 spring onions or 0.5 onion, sliced|spring_onion
- 5|1 garlic clove, chopped|garlic
- 32|2 tbsp soy sauce|soy_sauce
- 13.5|1 tbsp oil (plus a little extra)|olive_oil
- 4.5|1 tsp sesame oil (optional, to finish)|sesame_oil

## Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Pour in the beaten eggs, scramble softly, then set aside.
2. Add a little more oil, then the onion, pepper and garlic. Stir-fry 2-3 min.
3. Add the cold rice, breaking up clumps, and fry 3-4 min until hot and slightly toasted.
4. Stir in the spinach until wilted, return the egg, add soy sauce and toss. Finish with sesame oil and serve.

## Notes
- Cold rice is key — fresh warm rice goes mushy. Spread it out to cool fast if needed.
- Add leftover chicken, peas, or corn — this is the clean-out-the-fridge meal.
- Equipment: one wok or large pan.
