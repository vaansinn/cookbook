## Prep ahead
- Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear
- Let it drain 15 min while you get the seasoning ready

## Ingredients
- 300|300 g short-grain sushi rice|sushi_rice_dry
- 90|6 tbsp bottled sushi seasoning|sushi_seasoning_bottled

## Instructions
1. Rinse the rice in 2-3 changes of water. Drain 15 min.
2. Cook with 330 ml water: bring to a boil, cover, simmer 12 min, then rest covered off the heat 10 min.
3. Fold the bottled seasoning through the hot rice with a cutting motion.

## Notes
- The bottled seasoning replaces measuring vinegar, sugar and salt separately — one bottle, one step.
- Equipment: one pot.
